Common options

OptionDefaultPurpose
control_levelPython: 0; R: requiredControl arm for treatment contrasts.
outcome_model"lasso"Learner for the conditional outcome mean.
treatment_model"lasso"Learner for treatment probabilities.
n_folds5Number of cross-fitting folds.
calibration_method"auto"Calibration method.
inference"jackknife"Confidence interval method.
conf_level0.95Confidence level.
jackknife_folds10Number V of jackknife deletion groups; independent of nuisance cross-fitting.
bootstrap_reps200Number of bootstrap replicates.

Calibration

calibration_method accepts "auto", "isotonic", "smooth_isotonic", or "none". Use "none" to fit the estimator without calibration.

Inference

inference accepts "jackknife", "bootstrap", or "wald". See confidence intervals for the role of each method.
