Calibrated DML

Lars van der Laan, Alex Luedtke, and Marco Carone. Doubly robust inference via calibration. The main reference for the estimator and its inferential guarantees.

Lars van der Laan, Ziming Lin, Marco Carone, and Alex Luedtke. Stabilized Inverse Probability Weighting via Isotonic Calibration. CLeaR, 2025. Isotonic calibration of inverse probability weights.

V-fold jackknife

Yi Li, Ashkan Ertefaie, and Mark van der Laan. The V-fold jackknife for semiparametric inference: variance estimation, confidence intervals, and simultaneous confidence bands. 2026.

Adaptive estimation

Lars van der Laan, Marco Carone, Alex Luedtke, and Mark van der Laan. Adaptive debiased machine learning using data-driven model selection techniques.

David Benkeser and Mark van der Laan. A Super-Efficient Estimator of the Average Treatment Effect. Statistical Science, 2019.

Robinson (1988) develops partially linear regression. Crump et al. (2009) and Li, Thomas, and Li (2019) study estimation with limited treatment overlap.
